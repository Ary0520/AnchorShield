# Integration: Programmatic Hedging

For DAOs or automated treasuries holding significant USDC/EURC on Stellar, you can programmatically buy protection without using the AnchorShield UI.

## The "Buy Protection" Flow

To acquire YES tokens (Insurance Cover), your bot or smart contract should execute the following flow:

### 1. Fetch Market
Call `get_market_contract(market_id)` on the Market Factory to get the current USDC depeg market address.

### 2. Lock Collateral
Invoke `mint_complete_set(your_address, 1000)` on the Insurance Market contract. 
*Requires you to authorize pulling 1,000 USDC.*
You now possess 1,000 YES tokens and 1,000 NO tokens.

### 3. Sell the NO Tokens (Yielding the Premium)
You only want the YES tokens. You must sell the NO tokens to underwriters on the order book.
Invoke `place_order`:
- `owner`: your address
- `is_buy`: false (selling)
- `price_bps`: e.g., 9800 ($0.98)
- `amount`: 1000

Once the order is filled, you have effectively paid $0.02 (200 bps) per YES token, locking in your downside protection!
